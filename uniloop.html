Managerial economics snapshot

Illustrative assumptions for the academic proposal — not audited forecasts.

Seller commission8%
Illustrative avg. order₹650
Contribution / order₹40
Break-even volume450/mo

Model logic: commission revenue ≈ 8% × transaction value. A conservative contribution estimate of ₹40/order is used after payment/support costs. With assumed fixed monthly operating cost of ₹18,000, break-even ≈ 18,000 ÷ 40 = 450 transactions/month.
